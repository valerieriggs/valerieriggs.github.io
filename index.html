About

I joined Morgan State University in 2015 and became Associate Professor in 2026. Before that, I taught English and developmental writing at the Community College of Baltimore County and middle school social studies and magnet science in Baltimore County Public Schools.

At Morgan, I teach undergraduate and graduate courses including Artificial Intelligence for Educators, a course I developed, along with Action Research, Science of Reading, and educational technology. I chair the university's Foundational AI Education Curriculum Subcommittee, serve on the President's Committee on Strategic Use of AI, and am affiliated faculty with the NSF-funded TRAILS Institute for Trustworthy AI. I also design AI professional development for Maryland school districts and serve on the Education & Workforce Development Committee of the Maryland Responsible AI Council.

I have chaired or served on five doctoral dissertation committees and supervise undergraduate and graduate student researchers.
